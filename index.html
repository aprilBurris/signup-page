Who We are?

We are an organization that teaches young lady's and gentlemen how to be their best selves. We want to help parents teach their children how to be women and men with class.

Your child is able to stay in the club until they are 18 years old.

This meeting will break down the club in more details, give the pricing and what each child needs for the club.

Meeting will be held via Zoom @ 6:00 PM Central Time

About the club

Photo by Mohamad Khosravi on Unsplash

Age Requirements:

9 to 17 years old

What your child will learn:

  • Confidence and high self-esteem
  • Social & Table Etiquette
  • Manners
  • Cooking
  • Art
  • Class & personal grooming
  • Life skills & money management
  • Computer Science
  • French or Spanish
  • Ballroom Dancing
  • Formal Dancing

Each year the graduates will be given a formal dance.

Each group of graduates will take a trip to a place they all agree on.

Each age group will perform a ballroom dance at the end of each year to show off their skills.

More details will be given at the information meeting.
